	Proof of Delivery. A paper delivery note signed by the receiver. The driver takes a photo of it. It is not a digital signature

	Estimated Time of Arrival. It is only an estimate, shown only to staff

	House Air Waybill. The air transport document of one shipment, issued by the forwarder. Its number is entered on the order. It is not the waybill (the road transport document of a trip).
	GPS

	API
	Application Programming Interface
	LTL
	Less-Than-Truckload. Several small orders share one truck
	B2B

	SLA
	Service Level Agreement. Here, Dispatcher SLA is the time from Ready for Planning to truck assignment
	VND
	Vietnamese Dong
	OrderID
	The unique code of an order. Notes, photos, POD and documents are linked to it
	Tracking link
	A public link to one order. It uses a random token. Customers open it without an account
	Chargeable weight
	The weight used to choose the truck size: the larger of the actual weight and L × W × H (cm) / 5000
	Transit warehouse
	An ALS warehouse where the truck stops between pickup and drop-off, for example the My Dinh customs checkpoint
	Extra fee
	A fee added in the field after the price was agreed, for example for waiting time or extra loading work
	Collection-on-behalf fee
	Money that ALS pays to a third party for the customer and gets back later. It is not ALS revenue and has no VAT
	Vendor tracking code
	The tracking code that a truck rental company gives when ALS hands an order to it
	Major incident
	An incident that the Dispatcher must send to the Manager: damaged or lost cargo, a traffic accident, or cargo held by authorities
	

I. Overall Requirements
1. Context Diagram
The Context Diagram provides an overview of the NEXLOG system and shows how the system interacts with external actors and services.
The main actors are Manager, Dispatcher, Driver, Administrator, and Guest. The system also interacts with external services such as Google Maps Platform, Cloudinary, the Vehicle GPS System, the AI service (Google Gemini) and the email service to support logistics tracking and delivery operations.
  

Figure 1. Context Diagram of NEXLOG System
2. Main Business Flow
The five business flows below follow an order from intake to closing. They also cover vendor trucks, incidents and cancellations. Grey rounded boxes are actor steps. Yellow italic boxes are system steps. Orange diamonds are decisions. Dashed boxes are steps done outside the system. Dotted arrows are requests to the system and its answers.
2.1 Order Intake (BF-01)
Trigger: A customer fills in the order form on the website.
End condition: The order is Ready for Planning: the customer is confirmed and the documents are verified (go to BF-02).
  

Figure 2. Order Intake and Customs Documents (BF-01)
2.2 Trip Planning and Assignment (BF-02) 
Trigger: An order is Ready for Planning.
End condition: The trip is created and sent to the driver (go to BF-03).
  

Figure 3. Order Grouping and Truck Allocation (BF-02)
2.3 Trip Execution, Delivery and Closing (BF-03)
Trigger: The driver gets a new trip with its trip documents (end of BF-02).
End condition: The driver records any extra fees. The system sets the trip Completed and the truck driver available.  
Figure 4. Trip Execution, Delivery and Closing (BF-03)
2.4 Incident and Compensation Handling (BF-04)
Trigger: A driver reports an incident during trip (BF-03).
End condition: The trip goes on with the same truck, or with another truck or driver on the same trip, or the Manager closes the claim.
  

Figure 5. Incident and Compensation Handling (BF-04)
2.5 Order Cancellation (BF-05)
Trigger: A customer asks the Dispatcher to cancel an order.
End condition: If the cargo is already loaded, the order is locked and goes on to delivery (BF-03). Otherwise the truck is rerouted if it is already on its way, the Dispatcher agrees the cancellation fee with the customer and enters it, and the trip ends and its truck and drivers are released.
  

Figure 6. Order Cancellation and Compensation (BF-05)

3.2 Entity Descriptions
Entity: Order
Field
	Value
	Purpose
	Represents one transportation order of one customer, from pickup to delivery. 
	Key business attributes
	OrderID, HAWB number, Customer, Pickup Location, Delivery Location, Receiver name and phone, Source (Web, Hotline), Agreed price, Customer agrees to grouping, Responsible Dispatcher, Contract, Signed order contract file, Vendor, Vendor tracking code, Tracking token, Tracking token expiry, Document status, Cancellation reason, Cancellation fee, Is closed, Created by, Created time, Status.
	Business identity
	OrderID is unique. The tracking token is unique and unpredictable. The HAWB number comes from the documents of the customer (it is not generated by the system) and is not unique, because two forwarders may use the same number.
	Status / lifecycle
	Draft → Confirmed → Ready for Planning → Assigned → Accepted → Picked Up → In Transit → Delivered. A Draft is Confirmed only when it has the agreed price, the signed order contract and the HAWB number. While the order is Confirmed, its customer documents go Pending Documents → Documents Uploaded → Documents Verified. Other paths: Draft → Cancelled; Confirmed, Ready for Planning, Assigned or Accepted → Cancelled, until the driver records Loading Started at the pickup stop; Ready for Planning → Handed Over to Vendor. Closing is not a status: when an order becomes Delivered, Handed Over to Vendor or Cancelled, the system sets Is closed to Yes and the order is locked; its status stays the same (BR-19).
	Entity: Order Cargo
Field
	Value
	Purpose
	Stores the cargo of one order: what is carried, how much and how big. One cargo line for each order.
	Key business attributes
	OrderID, Cargo type (Pallet, Bulk cargo, General goods, Fragile electronics, High-value goods, Other), Cargo name, Quantity, Unit, Weight, Length, Width, Height, Specification, Stackability, Declared value, Special request note. Length, width and height are required for Pallet; Bulk cargo has only the weight and the specification (for example “40 cartons”). The cargo type is a classification only; it does not limit which truck is chosen.
	Business identity
	OrderID is unique: one cargo line for each order.
	Status / lifecycle
	No status. The cargo cannot be edited after the pickup (BR-32).

A file added for a type that is not required is Uploaded with Is required = No. Files are purged after the retention period counted from the upload time (JOB-01). A wrong file may be deleted before it is confirmed.

3.3 Data Business Rules
Rule
	Description
	Enforced On
	Unique username and email
	Two user accounts cannot have the same username or email address.
	Creating or updating a user account
	Unique customer tax code
	Two customers cannot have the same tax code in the system.
	Adding or updating a Customer
	Random tracking token
	Each order is assigned a cryptographically random, unguessable tracking token. The public tracking link never exposes the plain OrderID.
	Creating an order
	Price, contract and HAWB before confirming
	An order from the order form or entered by the Dispatcher can be Confirmed only when it has the agreed price, the signed order contract and the HAWB number.
	Updating Order to Ready for Planning
	Documents are not edited
	Uploaded customer files can be deleted before they are confirmed, but cannot be modified once uploaded or confirmed.
	Uploading / Confirming Customer Documents
	Differentiated document retention
	Customer documents (declarations, D/O) are permanently purged 60–90 days after order closure. Operational proofs (lead-seal photos, signed PODs, trip events) must be archived for at least 2 years, unless held by an active compensation claim.
	Purging Expired Documents & Background Jobs
	No manual swap on assigned trips
	Once a Trip is Assigned, the truck or the driver cannot be changed by hand. Any change must be linked to an Incident with a reason (UC-15).
	Assigning / Replacing Truck & Driver
	One order, one truck per trip
	An order must be transported entirely on a single truck per trip; order payload cannot be split across multiple vehicles.
	Trip Planning
	One unfinished trip per truck and driver
	An internal truck or driver can be assigned to only one unfinished trip (Assigned, Dispatched, Accepted or In Progress) at any given time.
	Assign Truck & Driver
	Approved vendor selection only
	Dispatchers can only assign orders or trips to external Vendors that have been formally reviewed and marked as "Approved" by a Manager. 
	Handing Over to Vendor/ Assigning Vendor Trip
	Vendor orders keep only the tracking code
	An order handed over to an external 3PL vendor only stores the vendor identity and vendor tracking code; driver apps, live seal verification, and internal GPS tracking are omitted.
	Hand Over to Vendor
	One contract party
	Each transport contract must link to exactly one Customer or exactly one Vendor entity.

II. Use Case Specifications
1. Order Management
1.1 Submit Order Form
Primary Actors
	Guest
	Secondary Actors
	Google Maps Platform
	Description
	As a customer, I want to send my orders through a link without an account, so that ALS gets the full order data at once.
	Preconditions
	The Guest has a valid order form link of a Dispatcher (BR-44).
	Normal Flow
	1. Guest opens the order form link.
2. System shows the Order Form with one empty order.
3. Guest enters the company details: company name, contact person, phone and email. The tax code and the registered address are optional here; the Dispatcher completes them before confirming.
4. Guest enters the order: pickup address, delivery address, cargo type, cargo name, quantity, unit, weight, stackability, receiver name and phone. For Pallet the Guest also enters the length, width and height; for Bulk cargo the specification. The Guest can add the HAWB number, the declared value and a special request note.
5. Guest clicks Add Order to enter another order, or goes to step 6.
6. Guest clicks Send.
7. System checks each field and the cargo rules: weight from 1 to 4,000 kg and no dangerous cargo (BR-38).
8. System matches the customer by tax code, or by email when no tax code is given. Otherwise it creates a customer (BR-44).
9. System finds the map position of each address (API-05) and matches it with the saved Locations.
10. System saves each order as Draft, gives it to the Dispatcher of the link (BR-20) and notifies that Dispatcher.
11. System shows MSG52.
	Alternative Flows
	Step 7a – Wrong data
System shows MSG02, MSG03 or MSG04 under the field. The form keeps the data.
Step 7b – Cargo not accepted
The weight is over 4,000 kg or the cargo is dangerous. System shows MSG04 and asks the Guest to call the Dispatcher.
Step 9a – Address not found
The order is still saved. The Dispatcher fixes the Location in UC-40 (MSG54).
Step 10a – Too many sends
System shows MSG37 (BR-44).
	Postconditions
	• The orders are Draft and linked to the customer and the Dispatcher.
• They have no price, no trip and no tracking link yet.
	 
1.2 Manage Orders
Primary Actors
	Dispatcher
	Secondary Actors
	Google Maps Platform
	Description
	As a Dispatcher, I want to check a draft order, agree the price and the order contract with the customer, and confirm the order, so that the customer can send the documents and the order can be planned.
	Preconditions
	• Dispatcher has logged in.
• A Draft order exists (sent by the customer on the website, or entered by the Dispatcher).
	Normal Flow
	1. Dispatcher opens a Draft order from the Order List.
2. System shows Order Details with the order data.
3. Dispatcher checks the data and the Locations, and edits them if needed. If the customer data is wrong, the customer corrects it on the form.
4. System runs UC-07 Calculate Route Distance & Time. It gets the route distance and driving time from Google Maps, adds the loading time and the breaks, and shows the trip time and the number of drivers (BR-04). For a trip over 8 hours the system suggests 2 drivers; the number of drivers is chosen at assignment (UC-09).
5. Dispatcher sets the required customer document types (BR-06). The Dispatcher completes the tax code, the registered address and the HAWB number if they are missing. Then the Dispatcher quotes the price to the customer by Zalo or Hotline, outside the system. In the same talk the Dispatcher asks if the order may join an open trip of the same customer (BR-05), and offers the document service: ALS prepares the customer documents for a fee (BR-30).
6. The customer agrees to the quote, says yes or no to the grouping, and chooses the document service or not, outside the system. If the customer agrees to grouping, the Dispatcher ticks Customer agrees to grouping.
7. Dispatcher enters the agreed price (BR-01). If the customer chose the document service, the Dispatcher adds its fee as an extra fee (BR-30).
8. Dispatcher clicks Create Contract. System makes the order contract from the order data and the agreed price (BR-45). The Dispatcher sends it to the customer, who signs it outside the system.
9. Dispatcher uploads the signed contract and clicks Confirm Order. System checks the agreed price, the signed contract and the HAWB number (BR-50) and asks to confirm (MSG57).
10. System sets the order to Confirmed, links it to the Active transport contract of the customer (BR-47) and creates the tracking token (BR-21). It emails the tracking link and the required document list to the customer and shows MSG08.
11. The customer uploads the customer documents (UC-02). If the customer chose the document service, the Dispatcher prepares and uploads them in UC-05. The Dispatcher then checks them in UC-05 Manage Customer Documents.
	Alternative Flows
	Step 1a – Order by phone
Dispatcher clicks New Order on the Order List, fills the Order Form for the customer and continues at step 2.
Step 4a – Distance service not available
System shows MSG34. The order stays Draft. The Dispatcher tries again later.
Step 5a – Documents not known (extension point)
UC-39 Ask Document Assistant. The Dispatcher adds the chosen documents to the list.
Step 5b – No document needed
The Dispatcher leaves the list empty. At step 10 the order becomes Ready for Planning at once.
Step 6a – The customer does not agree
Dispatcher clicks Cancel Draft. The order becomes Cancelled with no fee (BR-18).
Step 9a – Price, documents, signed contract or HAWB missing
System shows MSG02, MSG43, MSG67 or MSG68.
Step 10a – Email not sent

Customer (company and tax code), source (Web or Hotline), HAWB number, Locations, receiver, cargo (type, name, quantity and unit, weight, size or specification, stackable, declared value, special request note), agreed price, contract number, Dispatcher and tracking link. 	Number in VND, required to confirm. The price agreed with the customer outside the system (BR-01). It can change only before pickup (BR-32).

	Multi-select of document types: customs declaration, authorization letter, delivery order, bill of lading, sales contract, invoice, packing list, other. “Không cần chứng từ” (No document needed) is a tick box. One of them is required to confirm (MSG43). Hỏi trợ lý (Ask Assistant) opens the Document Assistant panel (BR-06).
	(7) Xác nhận đơn (Confirm Order)
	For a Draft. Works when the price, the HAWB number, the required documents and the signed contract are all there (BR-50; MSG02, MSG68, MSG43, MSG67) and asks to confirm (MSG57). The order becomes Confirmed. The system creates the tracking token and emails the tracking link and the required documents (MSG08). With no required documents, the order becomes Ready for Planning at once.
	(8) Các tab (Tabs)
	Customer Documents (default), Messages, Extra Fees, Trips & Evidence.
	(9) Bảng chứng từ khách hàng (Customer document table)
	The required types and the uploaded files: type, file, uploaded by, upload time and status (Uploaded, Confirmed or Rejected). View opens the file. Xác nhận (Confirm) accepts one file; Từ chối (Reject) asks for a reason and the customer sees it on the tracking page and uploads again. Delete removes a wrong file before confirmation. Files cannot be edited (BR-06). A Draft has no files yet: the customer uploads after the order is confirmed. The document deadline shows here after confirmation. Past it, the order is flagged (MSG41).
	(10) Tải lên thay khách (Upload on behalf)
	Works after the order is confirmed, for example when ALS prepares the documents for the customer. Opens a popup to choose the document type and a PDF, JPG, PNG, Excel or Word file of 10 MB or less (MSG09).
	(11) Xác nhận chứng từ (Confirm Documents)
	Works when each required type has a file (MSG11). Sets the documents to Documents Verified and the order to Ready for Planning (MSG12). The Dispatcher then assigns the truck and drivers.
	(12) Sửa đơn (Edit Order)
	Opens the Order Form. Cargo, price, HAWB and Locations can change only before pickup (BR-32, MSG33). A change that affects the trip runs the calculation again.
	(13) Thu hồi link (Revoke Link)
	Works after the order is confirmed (a Draft has no link). Asks to confirm (MSG63). Stops the old link, makes a new one and emails it. If the email fails, MSG32 shows the new link.
	(14) Bảng phí phát sinh (Extra fee table)
	Extra Fees tab. Adds a surcharge (including the document service fee) or a collection-on-behalf fee with amount and reason (BR-30). A suggested waiting fee becomes a fee only when the Dispatcher confirms it (BR-40).
	(15) Tab Chuyến & bằng chứng (Trips & Evidence)
	Read only. For each trip: the truck, the drivers, the stops, the events with time, the seal & plate photo, and the POD photo with the receiver name.
	(16) Tạo hợp đồng (Create Contract)
	For a Draft with an agreed price. Makes the order contract file from the order data and the agreed price. The Dispatcher sends it to the customer and the customer signs it, both outside the system. It is the contract of this order only, not the transport contract of the customer (BR-45).
	(17) Hợp đồng đã ký (Signed contract)
	File field. The Dispatcher uploads the signed order contract: a PDF, JPG or PNG file of 10 MB or less (MSG09). It is required to confirm the order (MSG67). It stays on the order.
	(18) Chọn xe & tài xế (Choose Truck & Driver)
	Button. Works only when the order is Ready for Planning. Opens Trip Assignment (BR-46).

11. Background Job Inventory
Trigger: Scheduled — every day at 2:00 AM
Purpose: Deletes files and GPS tracks when the retention period of their type is over.
Processing steps:
1. Read the retention period of each data type (BR-22 lists the types).
2. For each type, find the records that are past their period. For the files of an order, the period starts when the order is Delivered or Cancelled.
3. Skip orders that have a Pending compensation claim.
4. Delete each file in Cloudinary (API-03). Mark the file record as Purged. For POD and seal & plate photos, also delete the receiver name and the saved position. Keep the order data.
5. Delete the old GPS track points.
6. Log how many orders, files and rows were purged.
Failure behavior:
Scenario
	System behavior
	Cloudinary is not available or returns an error
	The file record does not change. The job tries again in the next run and logs a warning.
	The retention period of a type is not set
	The job deletes nothing of that type and logs a critical error.
	No old records
	The job ends. This is normal.
	11.2 Pull Truck Locations from GPS (JOB-02)
Trigger: Scheduled — every 1 minute
Purpose: Gets the location of trucks on active trips from the Vehicle GPS System. It is used for Trip Monitoring, the position check (BR-36) and Replay Trip.
Processing steps:
1. Find trucks with an Accepted or In Progress trip.
2. Call the Vehicle GPS Location API (API-04) with their truck IDs.
3. Save the latest position and its time for each truck. Add the point to the track of its trip.
Failure behavior:
Scenario
	System behavior
	The GPS API cannot be reached
	The last known position and its time are kept. Trip Monitoring shows "last updated". The job tries again in the next run. The track has a gap for that time.
	The provider does not know the truck
	The job skips the truck and logs a warning.
	11.3 Flag Orders Waiting Too Long (JOB-03)
Trigger: Scheduled — every 15 minutes
Purpose: Flags orders that stay in Ready for Planning longer than the set limit, for the Manager’s Order Workload.
Processing steps:
1. Read the limit.
2. Find orders in Ready for Planning longer than the limit and flag them.
3. Remove the flag from orders that have left Ready for Planning.
Failure behavior:
Scenario
	System behavior
	The database cannot be reached
	The job tries 3 times, 30 seconds apart, then logs a critical error. Flags are updated in the next good run.
	

11.4 Recalculate ETA (JOB-04)
Trigger: On event — a task is added at pickup and at each stop event. The worker checks for tasks every 10 seconds.
Purpose: Keeps the internal ETA (shown only to staff) and the late-risk flag up to date. Also updates the driving hours left of the driver.
Processing steps:
1. Take the oldest waiting task.
2. ETA: get the travel time from the phone position through the remaining stops (API-01). Add the stop time for each stop. It is the middle value of the last 10 visits at that Location. When there are fewer visits, use the setting. Save the ETA of each order (BR-13).
3. Late risk: flag an order when the ETA of its drop-off stop is more than 30 minutes later than the planned arrival of that stop.
4. Driving hours: calculate the driving hours left of the driver (BR-34).
5. Mark the task as done.
Failure behavior:
Scenario
	System behavior
	Google Maps Platform is not available
	The old ETA stays. The task tries again after 1, 2 and 4 minutes. After 5 failures the team gets an alert.
	Server restarts
	The task is still waiting and runs once after the restart.
	 
11.5 Clean Up Sessions and Notifications (JOB-05)
Trigger: Scheduled — every day at 3:00 AM.
Purpose: Deletes old sign-in sessions and old notifications, so the tables stay small.
Processing steps:
1. Delete sessions that expired or were signed out more than 7 days ago.
2. Delete notifications older than 90 days.
3. Log how many rows were deleted.
Failure behavior:
Scenario
	System behavior
	The database cannot be reached
	Nothing is deleted. The job runs again the next day.
	 
11.6 Remind Expiry Dates (JOB-06)
Trigger: Scheduled — every day at 6:00 AM.
Purpose: Warns the Manager before a truck inspection, a truck insurance or a driver licence expires. It also stops the use of a truck whose inspection has expired.
Processing steps:
1. Read the warning period (a setting).
2. Find trucks whose inspection or insurance, and drivers whose licence expires within the warning period.
3. Send one alert to the Manager for each item. Do not repeat the same alert on the same day.
4. Set a truck whose inspection has expired to Inactive.
5. Log how many alerts were sent.
Failure behavior:
Scenario
	System behavior
	The database cannot be reached
	Nothing changes. The job runs again the next day.

	2.3 Security
Requirement
	Description
	Sign-in required
	Every page and action needs sign-in, except the sign-in page and the public tracking page.
	Role-based access
	Each role can do only the actions allowed in the Permission Matrix. The server checks this on every request. A denied action shows MSG30.
	Password protection
	Passwords are never stored in a form that a person or a program can read. A new password has at least 8 characters (MSG59).
	Tracking link protection
	The tracking token cannot be guessed. It shows only its own order, with no data of other orders or customers. The page sends the header Referrer-Policy: no-referrer and the header X-Robots-Tag: noindex. The token stops working when the Dispatcher revokes the link (BR-21).
	File access
	Customer documents and photos can be opened only by staff who can view the order. Files are private. They open with a signed link that works for a short time. The system makes the link after it checks the right to view.
	Sign-in failure
	A failed sign-in message does not say which field was wrong.
	Audit trail
	The activity log records who created, changed, cancelled or closed orders, assignments, incidents and claims, and when. It also records the reason for a change to a locked trip.
	2.4 Reliability
Requirement
	Description
	Offline-first Driver PWA
	All driver actions work without a signal. They are sent without data loss when the signal comes back. Each action has its own ID, so a repeated send never makes a double record.
	External service failure
	If Google Maps Platform, Cloudinary, the GPS supplier or the AI service is down, the main order flow still works. Only the affected feature waits and tries again. If the AI service is down, the Dispatcher chooses the documents by hand.
	Backup
	The database is backed up every day. A backup can be restored within 4 hours.
	2.5 Data Retention and Privacy
Requirement
	Description
	Data retention
	Each data type has its own retention period (BR-22). Customer documents: 60–90 days. Seal & plate photos, POD photos and incident photos: 2 years. Waybills and trip events: 3 years. GPS tracks: 90 days. Order records without files are kept for reports.
	Personal data
	The system keeps customer contacts, receiver names and phones, signatures on POD photos and driver positions. It follows the Law on Personal Data Protection (Law 91/2025/QH15) and Decree 356/2025/ND-CP. It collects only the data needed for delivery.
	Data sent to the AI service
	The cargo type, cargo name, HAWB number and provinces of one order go to a third party. No personal data of the customer or the receiver is sent. The company names this use in its terms with customers. It also signs a data agreement with the AI provider.
	Driver position
	The system saves the phone position only with a stop event, a pickup or a delivery. It does not follow the phone all the time. Truck tracks come from the GPS supplier.
	Public page data
	The tracking page shows only the POD of that order. It does not show the customer tax code, contract data or prices. The receiver name is shown with some letters hidden.

	Trip, Driver shift, Driver profile
	Drivers need rest, cannot run two trips at the same time and must be allowed to drive the truck.
	System filters the driver list

	Drivers cannot reject a trip in the system. Accept means the driver has seen the trip.
	Trip
	Dispatching decisions stay with ALS staff.

	System

	Proves that the cargo left sealed.
	System, in Record Cargo Pickup
	BR-12

	Proof of delivery.
	System, in Record Delivery
	BR-13
	The ETA is not shown before pickup. It is calculated at pickup and again at each leg update or stop event, for each order. The stop time at a Location is the median of its last 10 visits. With fewer than 3 visits, the stop time setting is used. If the ETA of a drop-off stop is more than 30 minutes later than its planned arrival, the order is flagged as late risk. The ETA is an estimate and is shown only to staff, never on the tracking page.
	Order, Location
	Google Maps travel time does not include the time spent at stops. This time is different at each Location.
	System
	BR-14
	Customers see five milestones: Order received; Truck assigned (from Assigned); Picked up; Arrived at transit warehouse; Delivered. The transit milestone is shown only when the trip has a transit stop.

	Customers need plain steps, not internal statuses. The transit step is shown only when the cargo really goes through a transit warehouse.
	System
	BR-15
	For an order given to a vendor, the system keeps only the vendor and the vendor tracking code. There is no seal photo, POD or ETA in the system, and the order is not counted in fleet numbers.
	Order, Dashboard
	Vendors do not use the system, so it has no data from them.
	System
	BR-16
	A major incident is damaged or lost cargo, a traffic accident, or cargo held by authorities. The Dispatcher must send it to the Manager.
	Incident
	These cases can cost a lot of money or have legal effects, so a person with more authority must decide.
	Dispatcher
	BR-17
	Every compensation claim, from an incident or a cancellation, stays Pending until the Manager approves or rejects it.
	Compensation claim
	Compensation is money that ALS pays out, so one person with authority checks every claim.
	System
	BR-18
	An order can be cancelled while it is Confirmed, Ready for Planning, Assigned or Accepted, until the driver records Loading Started at its pickup stop. After that it goes on to delivery and the trip ends Completed. Before pickup, the system suggests a cancellation fee from the tiers of the contract linked to the order (BR-47) (a percent of the agreed price, by the hours before the planned start). The Dispatcher can change the amount. A trip with no orders left becomes Cancelled only before the truck starts.
	Order, Trip, Transport contract
	After pickup the company has spent money and the cargo must go back to a safe place. The contract sets what the customer pays.
	System, Dispatcher
	BR-19
	The system closes an order when it becomes Delivered or Cancelled, and when it is Handed Over to Vendor. A closed order is locked. The Dispatcher gives the contract file to the Finance department, outside the system.
	Order
	Payment is handled by the Finance department outside the system.
	System
	BR-20
	The Dispatcher who creates an order is its responsible Dispatcher until the Manager moves it. This Dispatcher is the contact on the tracking page. Any Dispatcher on shift can still work on the order.
	Order
	The customer has one Dispatcher to call.
	System
	BR-21
	The tracking link uses a random token that cannot be guessed. It never uses the plain OrderID. One token shows only its own order. The token stops working when the retention period of the POD photo ends, or when the Dispatcher revokes the link.
	Tracking page
	The page needs no login and shows customer data, so nobody may reach it by guessing an order number. A link shared by mistake can be stopped.
	System
	BR-22
	Each data type has its own retention period (a setting), counted from Delivered or Cancelled. Customer documents: 60–90 days. Seal & plate, POD and incident photos: 2 years. Files stay while a claim is Pending. Deleting a POD or seal & plate photo also deletes the receiver name and position. Order data is kept.
	Files, Waybill, Trip event, GPS track
	Customer documents hold customer data. Photos are evidence for claims up to 2 years (Commercial Law 2005, Article 319). A waybill is kept for 3 years (Decree 158/2024/ND-CP, Article 8). GPS tracks are personal data, so they are kept short.
	System, JOB-01
	BR-23
	The driver gets the stops in visiting order with their addresses. The system does not give turn-by-turn directions.
	Trip
	Google Maps routes are for cars and can send trucks onto roads they may not use. Drivers know the truck routes.
	System
	BR-24
	The Manager adds, edits and removes trucks. A truck is reserved for the time window of its trip from the moment the trip is created. The system sets it to On Trip when the trip starts (In Progress) and back to Available when the trip is Completed or Cancelled. It sets a truck to Inactive when its inspection expires (JOB-06). Only the Manager sets Maintenance, Inactive or Available, but not Available while the inspection has expired. The Dispatcher can only view the status.
	Truck
	The list of free trucks stays correct without manual work. A truck with an expired inspection must not run.
	System, by role
	BR-25
	An order is “waiting too long” when it stays in Ready for Planning longer than the set limit.
	Order
	The Manager sees stuck orders early and moves them before the customer is affected.
	System, JOB-03
	BR-26
	A failed sign-in message must not say if the username or the password was wrong. A locked account cannot sign in.
	Sign-in
	Stops people from finding valid usernames.
	System
	BR-27
	Every Driver PWA action works offline. It keeps the phone time and is sent when the signal returns. The phone time is used when it is valid: not in the future, not before the trip was assigned, and within the phone clock limit (a setting) of the server time. Otherwise the server time is used and the action is flagged. If the trip changed while the phone was offline, the action is kept and the Dispatcher is alerted (MSG38).
	Driver PWA, Trip event
	There is no signal inside bonded warehouses. A wrong phone clock must not break the ETA or the tracking page.
	System
	BR-28
	The Manager creates and locks Dispatcher and Driver accounts. Only the Administrator manages Manager accounts and assigns roles.
	User account
	The Administrator is a technical role.
	System, by role
	BR-29
	An order goes whole on one truck on a trip. It is not split between trucks.
	Trip
	Keep one seal and one POD for each order.
	System, in Trip Planning
	BR-30
	An extra fee is a surcharge, a waiting fee or a collection-on-behalf fee. Surcharges and waiting fees are revenue of the company. Collection-on-behalf fees are not revenue and have no VAT. The two groups are reported separately.


	Extra fee
	Keeps VAT reports correct.
	Dispatcher; the system suggests waiting fees (BR-40)
	BR-31
	Customer documents must be PDF, JPG, PNG, Excel (XLS, XLSX) or Word (DOC, DOCX) files of 10 MB or less. The signed order contract must be a PDF, JPG or PNG file of 10 MB or less. Seal & plate photos and POD photos must be JPG or PNG files of 10 MB or less.
	Files
	Keeps upload time short on cellular networks. Customers send declarations and contracts as Excel or Word files.
	System
	BR-32
	The cargo, price, HAWB number and Locations of an order can be edited only before pickup. Every change goes into the activity log.
	Order
	The evidence stays consistent after the cargo is sealed.
	System
	BR-33
	The total actual weight on a truck must not be more than its allowed payload (equal is allowed). The system checks it when a truck is assigned or changed, and when the driver records the loaded weight at pickup. Only trucks that can carry the weight are suggested (BR-03). There is no button to go over the limit.
	Trip, Truck, Trip stop
	Overloading is fined (Decree 168/2024/ND-CP) and is not safe.
	System, in Suggest Truck Size, Assign Truck & Driver to Trip and Record Cargo Pickup
	BR-34
	A driver must not drive more than 4 hours without a break, 10 hours in one day or 48 hours in one week. The system counts driving time from Left to Arrived. A break stops the count. It does not offer a driver with too few hours left (BR-08), warns the Manager about a longer shift, and alerts the Dispatcher near 4 hours (MSG65). The limits are set by law, so the settings cannot change them.
	Driver profile, Driver shift, Trip event
	Law 36/2024/QH15 (Article 64) sets these limits for commercial drivers. A tired driver is a risk for people and cargo.
	System
	BR-35
	Every trip of a company truck has a waybill, made as a PDF when the truck and the driver are assigned. A replacement trip gets a new one. It shows the company name, truck plate, customer, start and end points, cargo type, total weight and, if any, the contract number and date. The driver opens it on the phone. The customer signs the printed copy to confirm the loaded weight. It is kept for 3 years (BR-22). A vendor order has no waybill (BR-15).
	Trip, Waybill
	Decree 158/2024/ND-CP (Article 8) requires the driver to carry the waybill and the company to keep it for at least 3 years.
	System
	BR-36
	When the driver records arrived, loading started, picked up or delivered, the system compares the phone position with the stop Location. If the distance is more than the geofence radius (a setting; a Location can have its own), the event is still saved, marked “outside radius”, and the Dispatcher is alerted (MSG50). The driver is never blocked. With no phone position, the event is saved without a check.
	Trip event, Location
	A wrong position can be a mistake or a false record, but a driver must not be stopped at a gate with bad signal.
	System
	BR-37
	A compensation claim should not be more than the limit of the order: the smaller of the contract liability cap and the declared value, or 500 million VND for each claim if there is neither. The system warns when the amount is higher (MSG56). The Manager can still approve it with a reason.
	Compensation claim, Transport contract, Order
	Without an agreement, Decree 163/2017/ND-CP limits the liability of a logistics business to the declared value (500 million VND for each claim if none).
	System, Manager
	BR-38
	Dangerous cargo are not accepted.
	Order
	These cargo types need special trucks, permits and trained drivers.
	Dispatcher
	BR-39
	Each drop-off ends with the result Delivered. It needs the POD photo (BR-12); the receiver name is optional. When the driver cannot hand over the cargo, for example the receiver is not there, the driver reports an incident and the Dispatcher handles it with the customer (BF-04). The order stays In Transit until the delivery is saved.
	Trip stop, Order
	The customer and the company need to know what really happened at each drop-off, and cargo must not get lost.
	System, Dispatcher
	BR-40
	Waiting time is counted from Arrived to Loading started. When it is longer than the free waiting time in the contract, the system suggests a waiting fee. It uses the waiting fee rate of the contract. The suggestion becomes an Extra Fee only when the Dispatcher confirms it. If the driver skips Loading started, no fee is suggested.
	Trip event, Extra fee, Transport contract
	A truck that waits costs money. A fee that the system suggests and a person confirms is fair to the customer.
	System, Dispatcher
	BR-41
	A trip is locked from Assigned (BR-10). A change of its truck or driver needs a reason, and the reason goes to the activity log.
	Trip, Activity log
	A person is responsible for every change to a locked trip. The reasons show later why a trip was changed.
	System
	BR-42
	Only the cargo type, the cargo name, the HAWB number and the provinces of the order are sent to the AI service. It returns the cargo class, a risk level and the suggested documents. No customer name, tax code, phone, email or receiver data is sent. The answer is only a suggestion and nothing is stored. If the AI service is off or fails, the Dispatcher chooses the documents by hand.
	Order
	The AI service gets only what it needs, and a person decides.
	System, Dispatcher
	BR-43
	The 3D load plan is a guide. The system places the orders of one trip in the truck box, one block for each order (its length × width × height). Bulk cargo has no size: it is not drawn, only listed with its weight, and its weight still counts in the payload. The order delivered last goes deepest, near the front wall. The order delivered first goes nearest the door. A block goes on top of another only when the lower order is Stackable, the upper order is delivered earlier or at the same stop, and the pile stays under the box height. A block that does not fit is marked and MSG66 shows. The plan is calculated when the screen opens. It is not saved and never blocks an assignment or a pickup.
	Trip, Truck, Order
	Loading in the reverse order of the stops saves handling at each drop-off. A simple guide is enough: the driver and the loader decide the real loading.
	System
	BR-44
	Each Dispatcher has an order form link. A Guest sends orders through it without login. The orders are saved as Draft for the Dispatcher of the link. A known tax code, or a known email when no tax code is given, links the order to the saved customer; otherwise a new customer is created. The Dispatcher completes the tax code before confirming. The system limits how often the link can be used.
	Order, Customer
	The customer sends the full order data at once without an account. The limit stops abuse of a public link.
	System
	BR-45
	The order contract is made for one order from the order data and the agreed price. The customer signs it outside the system and the Dispatcher uploads the signed file. It is the contract of this order only, not the transport contract of the customer. An order cannot be confirmed without the signed file.
	Order
	The price and the terms of each order are agreed in writing before work starts.
	Dispatcher; the system blocks Confirm Order
	BR-46
	A Draft order only gets the route distance and the trip time. The truck and the drivers are chosen only when the order is Ready for Planning (UC-09). If no fleet truck is free, the Dispatcher hands the order over to a vendor (BR-07).
	Order, Trip
	Documents can still be missing, so the plan can change before the order is ready.
	System, Dispatcher
	BR-47
	When an order is confirmed, the system links it to the Active transport contract of its customer. The link does not change afterwards. The fee tiers, the waiting terms and the liability cap of that contract are used for the order. If the customer has no Active contract, nothing is suggested and the Dispatcher enters the amount.
	Order, Transport contract
	The terms at the time of confirming stay with the order.
	System
	BR-48
	Only Published news posts are shown on the website, newest first. Only the Manager writes, publishes, unpublishes and deletes posts. An unpublished or deleted post is hidden from the website.
	News Post
	The website must show only content the company approved.
	System
	BR-49
	A password reset link works once and expires after 30 minutes (System Setting). The reset screen shows the same message whether the email exists or not. After a reset, all other sessions of the account are signed out.
	User Account
	Protects accounts and does not reveal which emails have accounts.

	BR-50
	A Draft order can be Confirmed only when it has the agreed price, the signed order contract and the HAWB number.
	Order
	These are needed to issue the tracking link, link the contract and check the customs documents.
	System, on Confirm Order
	BR-51
	Every status change of an order or a trip is recorded with the new status, the user (or the system) and the time.
	Order, Trip
	Gives a full history for disputes and for the Manager.
